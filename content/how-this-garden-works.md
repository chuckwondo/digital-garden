*A short guide to how these notes are grown, staged, and linked.*

This is a digital garden: a collection of interlinked notes and essays, organized by idea rather than by date. Notes are meant to grow over time, not to be finished. Each one is planted rough and tended as it develops, so you will find them at every stage of maturity.

## Growth stages

Every note carries a stage marking how settled it is:

- 🌱 **seedling:** rough, just planted; may be incomplete or wrong.
- 🌿 **budding:** developing and taking shape; still evolving.
- 🌳 **evergreen:** mature and stable (but never truly done).

## How notes connect

Notes link to each other liberally. A link sometimes points to a note that does not exist yet: that is a planting marker for something worth writing later, not a mistake. Wander by following links or browsing by tag rather than looking for a linear order.

## Work in progress

Some notes carry a STATUS block near the top, recording what is settled and what is still open. Read those as honest signposts: the note is being actively worked, and its shape may still change.
