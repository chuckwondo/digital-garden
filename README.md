# 🌱 Digital Garden

A personal, evolving collection of interlinked notes and essays — tended over
time and kept in whatever state they happen to be in. Not a blog: organized by
*idea*, not by date, and notes are meant to grow rather than ever be "finished."

> This is my **digital** garden — writing, ideas, and technical notes. My
> **physical** home and garden live in a separate `home-and-garden` repo.

## Growth stages

Each note carries a stage marking how settled it is:

- 🌱 **seedling** — rough, just planted; may be incomplete or wrong
- 🌿 **budding** — developing and taking shape; still evolving
- 🌳 **evergreen** — mature and stable (but never truly done)
